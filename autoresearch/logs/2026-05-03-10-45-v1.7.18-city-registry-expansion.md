# AutoResearch Run — 2026-05-03 10:45 UTC — Track D city-registry expansion

## Hypothesis

The city registry at v1.7.12 (387 cities) had documented gaps from
Reviewer E's gap analysis (`autoresearch/proposals/v1.7.8-reviewer-e-gaps.md`)
plus other under-represented metros: Pakistani provincial centers, Indian
non-capitals, Iranian cities, Saudi/UAE secondary cities, Egyptian Nile-Delta
cities, Indonesian provincial capitals, Türkiye top-population provinces,
European diaspora metros (Stuttgart, Naples, Sevilla, Leeds, etc.), American
metros (Boston, San Francisco, Seattle, etc.), and Latin American diaspora
hubs (Foz do Iguaçu, Curitiba, Maracaibo).

Adding these closes detection gaps where users in non-capital, mid-sized
metros currently get a `country-default` method-source dispatch without
city-level provenance — and lets `nearestCity()` return a meaningful label
rather than the country capital.
