## Change made

### `src/engine.js`

1. **Syria's lat upper bound** tightened from 37.4 to 37.05 (Gaziantep TR
   precondition; Aleppo SY 36.20 still inside).
2. **Türkiye coverage** added in two strips, AFTER Caucasus to avoid
   Yerevan AM (40.18, 44.51) overlap:
   - Eastern: lat 37.05-42.10, lon 26.0-44.5 (Gaziantep, Van, Iğdır...)
   - Western: lat 36.0-37.05, lon 26.0-35.7 (Antalya, Mersin, Adana)
   - Engine canonical name is `Turkey` (English) per
     ISO_TO_ENGINE_COUNTRY_LOCAL — **not** `Türkiye`. selectMethod has
     both case `Turkey` and case `Türkiye` for backwards compat.
3. **Egypt's lat-min** tightened from 21 to 22 (1899 Anglo-Egyptian
   parallel). Wadi Halfa SD (21.81) now correctly falls to Sudan.
4. **Early Sudan check** added between Egypt and Saudi Arabia. Port Sudan
   (19.62, 37.22) was caught by Saudi Arabia's bbox 16-33 / 35-56.
5. **Netherlands BEFORE Belgium AND BEFORE France**. Eindhoven NL (51.44,
   5.45) was caught by Belgium; Maastricht NL (50.85, 5.69) by France.
   NL-first claims the overlap zone.
6. **Malaysia's lat-min** tightened from 0.5 to 1.0 — northern Sumatra
   (Pekanbaru 0.51) excluded; Johor Bahru 1.49 still inside.
7. **Afghanistan split into main + Wakhan corridor**:
   - Main: lat 29.4-37, lon 60.5-71.5 (Kabul, Kandahar, Mazar-e Sharif)
   - Wakhan: lat 36.5-38.5, lon 71.5-74.95
   Pakistan now safely extends to lon 75.0 covering Sialkot (74.52)
   without conflicting with Afghan main territory.
8. **Pass-B coverage** added for Turkey, Indonesia, Netherlands, Sudan
   in `COUNTRY_BBOX_TABLE`. Names match `ISO_TO_ENGINE_COUNTRY_LOCAL`
   keys (English `Turkey`).
