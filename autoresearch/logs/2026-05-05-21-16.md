# AutoResearch Run — 2026-05-05 21:16 UTC

## Hypothesis

Initial hypothesis (rejected): The v1.5.0 +5 Maghrib and v1.7.16 +5 Dhuhr Path A
buffers were tuned to mosque-aggregated Mawaqit data, not the Moroccan Ministry
of Habous (the institutional religious authority). Two Moroccan users on Twitter
cited Habous as their trusted reference. Therefore the country-default Morocco
dispatch should flip to "Habous-aligned" (no +5 buffers, ministerial astronomical),
with `method: 'MoroccoMawaqit'` retained as a community-aligned opt-in for apps
that prefer mosque-published reality.

Refined hypothesis (accepted): Empirical re-check against the Habous Ministry
**direct API** (`habous.gov.ma/prieres/horaire-api.php`, 12 cities × 2026-05-05)
shows Habous itself publishes Dhuhr ~+5 min after astronomical noon AND Maghrib
~+5 min after astronomical sunset — the muwaqqit-tradition zawal- and sunset-
ihtiyati that Moroccan ministerial astronomy bakes into its published Imsakiyya.
The v1.5.0 + v1.7.16 Path A buffers therefore ALREADY track Habous (and Mawaqit
agrees with Habous on the same +5). There is no Mawaqit/Habous ikhtilaf for fajr
to surface. Default stays at +5; `MoroccoMawaqit` and `MoroccoHabous` become
same-as-default semantic aliases for caller UX clarity.

## Verdict

**ACCEPTED** — restored v1.7.16 +5 Maghrib + +5 Dhuhr default, retained
MoroccoMawaqit / MoroccoHabous as semantic aliases pointing to the same calc.

The Layer 4 design instinct ("Habous is first-class, so default to Habous") was
**correct in spirit** and incorrect in implementation premise. The empirical
re-check shows that "default to Habous" and "default to v1.7.16 +5 buffers"
are the same outcome — Habous bakes the +5 into its Imsakiyya at the muwaqqit-
calculation layer. The flip-then-revert is captured here so future maintainers
checking the design tension see the empirical resolution rather than re-running
the same flip.
