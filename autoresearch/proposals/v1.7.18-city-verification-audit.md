# v1.7.18 city-verification audit (on-disk methodology, 2026-05-03)

Master at v1.7.15 — read-only Track E redo of the registry-quality audit. The
previous attempt hit a content-filter rejection while WebFetching demographic
pages; this redo is mechanical and uses only on-disk sources.

Source files audited (all read-only):

- `src/data/cities.json` — 387 city entries (the previously-cited "375" was
  approximate; current registry has exactly 387 rows including two intentional
  duplicates: Jerusalem IL/PS and Tripoli LY/LB).
- `scripts/data/mawaqit-mosques.json` — 409 active mawaqit slugs covering 211
  unique city names.
- `scripts/data/city-method-overrides.json` — 16 verified city-level
  institutional method overrides.
- `src/engine.js` — `detectLocation()`/`detectCountry()` bbox table consulted
  via dynamic ESM import.

## Methodology

Each of the 387 cities was scored on four binary dimensions, then mapped to a
verification tier. The fifth dimension (coordinate accuracy by ground-truthed
geocoder) is intentionally skipped — it requires WebFetch.

| Dim | Check | On-disk source |
|-----|-------|----------------|
| **C2** Country attribution | `detectLocation(city.lat, city.lon).country` matches the engine country corresponding to `city.countryISO` | `src/engine.js` (bbox table); `ISO_TO_ENGINE_COUNTRY_LOCAL` mirror |
| **C3** Mosque anchor | Any active Mawaqit slug whose `city` field name-matches this city (or, equivalently, whose slug appears in the city's `source.slug`) | `scripts/data/mawaqit-mosques.json` |
| **C4** Institutional method anchor | The city's normalised name appears as a key in the city-level overrides registry | `scripts/data/city-method-overrides.json` |
| **C5** Source-field accuracy | The city's `source.type` is consistent with what C3 + C4 actually saw: `mawaqit` ⇔ C3 holds; `national-authority` ⇔ accepted (country-level institutional, no per-city contradiction); `inherited` / `fallback` ⇔ neither C3 nor C4 holds | `src/data/cities.json` |

### Tier scheme

- **Verified (green)** — C2 passes AND (C3 or C4 holds) AND C5 holds. The
  city has either a Mawaqit anchor or an explicit institutional override AND
  its `source` field correctly advertises that anchor.
- **Approaching verified (yellow→green)** — C2 passes AND (C3 or C4 holds),
  but C5 fails: the city has an anchor but advertises itself as `inherited`
  or otherwise mis-classifies its source. One-line metadata fix.
- **Partial (yellow)** — C2 passes but neither C3 nor C4 holds. The city
  inherits its country-default method via `methodForCountry()`. This is the
  "Aladhan-aligned, citation pending" tier.
- **Unverified (red)** — C2 fails: the city's claimed `countryISO` does not
  match `detectCountry`'s verdict.

## What this audit does NOT verify

The methodology intentionally skips dimensions that require WebFetch or
external geocoding:

- **C1 Coordinate accuracy** — whether `(city.lat, city.lon)` is actually
  the canonical centroid of the named city per a third-party geocoder. We
  trust the registry; on-disk Pass-A/Pass-B reachability is verified, but
  not the absolute coord.
- **Mosque-coord proximity (haversine)** — the spec called for "mosque
  within 10 km via haversine." Mawaqit slugs in `mawaqit-mosques.json` carry
  no `lat`/`lon` field — only `city`/`country`/`region`. The C3 check
  therefore reduces to city-name matching against the slug's `city` field.
  This is approximate but on-disk-faithful; a future Track-B run could add
  lat/lon to mawaqit-mosques.json by parsing slug-embedded city centroids.
- **Method-dispatched-time vs published-time** — i.e. whether the engine's
  computed Fajr at Karachi matches the Karachi mosque's published Fajr to
  within minutes. That is the WMAE eval's job, not this registry audit.

A v1.7.19 follow-up could re-run this audit with an external geocoder
allowed (Nominatim / OSM) to validate C1, expanding coverage to all five
dimensions.
